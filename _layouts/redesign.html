Research focus

From the known to the unknown.

My research develops AI systems that infer the unknown from the known, recovering hidden visual structures with vision-language models, imputing missing Earth observations, and forecasting future states from past dynamics.

CV

Amodal Perception and Reasoning

Using vision-language models to infer complete object structures and reason about complex occlusions beyond visible evidence.

  • Amodal segmentation and reasoning under complex occlusion
  • VLM-guided shape completion from weak visual and language cues
AI

Earth Observation

Reconstructing missing Earth observations under cloud occlusion and forecasting future environmental states from known spatial and temporal context.

  • Climate and Earth observation imputation under large-scale missingness
  • Weather-field and long-term time-series forecasting
